## How the simulation works

> **Draft.** To be reviewed by the team.

1. **Choose a model.** Start with a simple sphere or a two-lobe brain.
2. **Set up the blow.** Pick a preset or adjust the punch speed, the direction of the blow and
   the properties of the brain tissue.
3. **Strike.** Watch the shockwave travel through the tissue in slow motion.
4. **Read the results.** Compare peak force, strain and pressure in the statistics.

### The simulation window

- **Rotate** the view by dragging, and **zoom** with the mouse wheel or a pinch.
- The **axis gizmo** in the top-right corner shows the orientation of the head. Click an axis to
  look along it. The axes are the head coordinate frame described in
  [Spherical coordinates](#spherical-coordinates).
- The **timeline** at the bottom records every frame while the simulation runs. Drag it, or step
  one frame at a time, to look at any moment in detail.
- The **expand** button turns the window into a full-page view; press Esc to leave it.
